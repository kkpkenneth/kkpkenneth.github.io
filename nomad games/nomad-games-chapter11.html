Chapter 11

The Cradle of Passion

Stepping off the transit carriage, Kenjiro immediately felt the air shift from the artistic calm of the gardens to a wave of intense, dry warmth. This was the Cradle of Passion, a sun-baked land where terracotta rooftops met vibrant plazas, and the rhythmic sound of clapping flamenco heels reverberated through the afternoon heat.

"Whoa. The energy here is completely different," Kenjiro murmured, shielding his eyes from the blazing sun as he adjusted his bag. He glanced down at his empty pockets with a wry grin. "And true to form, our transit fare got us here, which means we are officially back to zero coins. Good thing experiencing this atmosphere doesn't cost a dime."

"Your lack of monetary reserves continues to impress me," Kame-kun whirred from his perch on Kenjiro's shoulder. "However, the thermal indices here are exceptionally high, and my sensors register a localized surge of intense emotional frequency nearby."

Following the sound of rapid, furious footwork and snapping guitars, they drifted into a bustling open-air pavilion. In the center of the courtyard, a fiery performer was locked in an intense dance, her heels striking the wooden stage with blistering speed. Sitting right beside the edge of the stage was another navigation AI—an ordinary, fluffy plush cat, watching the performance with deadpan digital focus.

"A 4/4 syncopated compás executed with 98% temporal precision," the plush cat pilot chimed in, its voice completely flat despite the passionate surroundings. "Though my partner's stamina is decaying faster than expected."

As the dancer struck a final, dramatic pose with her arms raised high, the crowd erupted into thunderous applause and tossed coins onto the stage. The dancer bowed deeply, breathless and radiant, before walking over to grab her guitar case.

"¡Olé! That is what I call living!" she laughed, wiping sweat from her brow and tossing her dark hair. She noticed Kenjiro and Kame-kun watching. "Ah, travelers! How do you find the Cradle of Passion?"

"It's blazing fun!" Kenjiro replied enthusiastically, smiling back at her vibrant energy. "I'm Kenjiro, and this is Kame-kun."

"Carmen," she introduced herself with a bright, confident grin, gesturing to the plush cat on her shoulder. "And this is my pilot, Sol. You don't just look at it with your eyes—you feel it in your bones! If you are going to survive the heat of this land on your global tour, you have to let the fire take over! In fact, why don't you join me for the next set as my tourist guests?"

Before Kenjiro could protest, Carmen grabbed his hand and pulled him onto the wooden stage. As the guitars struck up a lively rhythm, Kenjiro gamely clapped along and improvised a clumsy, enthusiastic tourist dance, trying his best to keep up.

Realizing his human companion was floundering, Kame-kun hopped down from his resting spot onto the stage. To everyone's absolute shock, the plush-sized turtle began executing surprisingly crisp, rhythmic spins and side-shuffles to the beat, his stuffed flippers moving with comical precision.

"Calibrating angular momentum to match regional tempo," Kame-kun deadpanned mid-spin, completely unfazed.

The crowd absolutely lost their minds. Shrieks of laughter and roaring applause filled the plaza as people went wild for the dancing turtle, raining a heavy shower of gold and silver coins onto the stage.

"¡Increíble! Look at you two go!" Carmen cheered, scooping up a handful of the tossed coins and pressing them directly into Kenjiro's pockets once the routine ended. "You earned every bit of that extra cash for your world tour, dancing tourist!"

"I think Kame-kun carried our entire performance," Kenjiro laughed, wiping his forehead and listening to the satisfying jingle of heavy coins in his pocket.

"Now take that reward down to the tavern," Carmen winked, picking up her case. "Treat yourselves to a chilled pitcher of sangria, some sizzling plates of gulas al ajillo, a massive pan of seafood paella, and a refreshing bowl of gazpacho before you move on to the Haven of Gourmet!"
