Decoding the world through history, culture, and travel.

The Journey So Far

I am Kenneth, a global nomad originally from Hong Kong. What started as a standard pursuit of travel has evolved into a lifelong mission to document the intersections of history, endemic botany, and regional cultures across the globe.

Currently writing from the ancient Silk Road routes of Baku, Azerbaijan, I use this space to dive deep into traditional discovery. Whether I'm trekking through remote plateaus or studying the architectural nuances of UNESCO sites, my goal is to provide a deeper, educational perspective on global exploration.

Areas of Research

This platform serves as a public archive for my specialized interests, focusing on areas that go beyond standard tourism:

  • Endemic Botany: Documenting rare flora, from the Frankincense elongata on the Homhil plateau to the iconic Dragon blood trees of the Socotra Archipelago.
  • Heritage & Architecture: Analyzing the historical engineering of monuments like Mahabalipuram, the Taj Mahal, and the ruins of Nalanda University.
  • Culinary Anthropology: Tracing the roots of regional street food and the distillation processes of traditional spirits like Goan Urrak and Lebanese Arak.
