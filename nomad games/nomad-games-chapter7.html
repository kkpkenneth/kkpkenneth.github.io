Chapter 7

The Great Fire Wall

The journey led the duo to the base of a colossal, seemingly impenetrable barrier known as the Great Fire Wall. It was a massive architectural anomaly, glowing with a faint, heat-less red energy.

"There are no doors," Kenjiro muttered, walking along the perimeter and running his hand along the smooth, sheer surface. "The Great Fire Wall has no gates at all. The only way to get to the other side is to climb over the wall. But how do you even do that?"

Exhausted and out of ideas, Kenjiro groaned, giving up entirely. He slumped down and sat on the dirt floor, Kame-kun, the plush size AI turtle perching on his shoulder, emitting a soft, digitized hum of frustration.

Suddenly, with a soft thud, a local with a grappling hook just landed seamlessly next to them.

"You don't look like a local," the stranger said cheerfully, dusting off their robes. "Are you having trouble climbing over the fire wall? It's actually quite simple. You just need the right tools."

"Here you are," the local continued, tossing a heavy burlap sack at Kenjiro's feet. "Vines, Pitons, and a Nut tool. To get past the wall, you just need V, P, and N."

Equipped with the V, P, and N, Kenjiro painstakingly scaled the sheer face of the barrier. Finally, the duo climbed to the top of the wall and stood up, gazing out over the sprawling horizon.

"The land is enormous inside the wall," Kenjiro breathed in awe.

They spent an hour just walking around the wide parapets, indulging themselves in the spectacular scenery of mist-shrouded ridged mountains and cascading, crystalline waterfalls. After taking in the breathtaking view, the duo started climbing down the other side of the wall.

But the moment they crossed the inner edge of the wall, a sharp crackle of static filled the air. Kame-kun's optical sensors suddenly flickered, flashed bright red, and then died completely. He broke down, slipping off Kenjiro's shoulder and falling all the way to the ground with a dull thud.

"Kame-kun!" Kenjiro yelled, scrambling down the rest of the vines.

Before his boots even hit the dirt, an old lady darted out from a nearby thicket, scooped up the disconnected Kame-kun, and quickly ran away.

"Hey! You sure are fast given your age!" Kenjiro shouted, sprinting after her. He chased the old lady through a maze of stone pathways, finally cornering her at the back of her rustic home.

"This is my friend Kame-kun," Kenjiro panted, holding his hands out pleadingly. "Can you give me back my friend?"

"Sorry, finder's keepers," the old lady huffed, clutching the plush turtle tightly. "And I'm going to eat this delicious turtle!"

"Noooooooo!" Kenjiro cried in horror. "I'd do anything to help you, but please don't eat my turtle friend!"

Seeing his genuine desperation, the old lady's tough exterior crumbled. She ushered him inside, where a young boy lay pale and shivering on a bed. Then came the story of the old lady's sick grandson. It turned out that the old lady was trying to look for the mystic tea plant inside the walls, which rumor had it could cure any disease.

"But to find the plant, one has to scale up those jagged mountains for four hours," the old lady sobbed, wiping away tears. She blamed herself for not having a body healthy enough to help her grandson.

"I will get it for you," Kenjiro decided firmly, resolving to help the old lady. "But in return, you must give Kame-kun back to me safe and sound."

The scale up the mountain was tough, but the environment was bursting with life. Along the way, Kenjiro trekked through a dense bamboo forest where there lived many majestic creatures, including giant pandas munching lazily on stalks, agile red pandas leaping between branches, chattering golden monkeys swinging overhead, and massive giant salamanders resting in the cool mountain streams.

After four grueling hours, Kenjiro finally reached the misty peak and found the mystic tea plant. He carefully harvested its shimmering leaves and raced back down the mountain.

"To make tea, you carefully crush the fresh leaves, steep them in pure boiling spring water, and strain the liquid precisely three times," Kenjiro instructed as he worked over the stove.

When poured, this mystical tea, unlike usual tea, was perfectly transparent. Yet, it produced a wildly intense aroma, and when tasted, it offered multiple, evolving layers of rich, herbaceous flavors.

"My dear grandson, please drink the tea and feel better," the old lady whispered softly, lifting the cup to the boy's lips.

The grandson sipped the transparent tea slowly, and eventually drank the whole cup. Miraculously, the grandson's color returned, and he sat up, starting to feel much better. Overjoyed, the old lady started dancing around the room. In her wild celebration, she bumped the table and accidentally spilled the leftover tea from the teapot directly onto the offline Kame-kun.

"Uwaaaaaah! Bikkurishita!" Kame-kun shrieked in Japanese, his optical sensors snapping back online as he short-circuited into wakefulness.

"Kame-kun! You're back!" Kenjiro shouted, grabbing the wet, plush size AI turtle and pulling him into a massive, crushing hug.

"Waaaaah, Kenjiro, you are squeezing me too hard that I'm being squished!!!!!!" Kame-kun protested digitally.

"I'm glad you're back. I was so worried," Kenjiro laughed, finally loosening his grip.

"So what happened just then when I was offline?" Kame-kun asked, shaking the transparent tea off his chassis.

Kenjiro explained how he scaled the steep mountains all alone, navigated the bamboo forest, and brewed the mystic tea to help the old lady's grandson.

"So that's what happened," Kame-kun hummed. Out of pure curiosity, Kame-kun directed a medical scan at the newly recovered boy. The results chimed in his processor. "Fascinating. Kenjiro, my scans indicate that the grandson was just severely dehydrated. Literally any liquid would have helped him recover."

"So my hard work scaling a mountain for four hours was completely futile?!?" Kenjiro groaned, his shoulders dropping in disbelief.

"Sufficient exercising is always good for your health and mind," Kame-kun replied smoothly, perching himself back upon Kenjiro's shoulder.

The entire group burst into bright, echoing laughter. To show her immense gratitude, the old lady cooked them an incredible, ten-course thank you meal. With full stomachs and high spirits, Kenjiro and his plush AI companion stepped back out into the enormous land within the walls, ready for the journey to continue.
