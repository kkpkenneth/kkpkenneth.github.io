Chapter 5.1

The Borderlands

Kenjiro navigated the desolate, heavily fortified perimeter separating the static elegance of the Infinity Palace from the towering, mechanized skyline of the Robot Metropolis. A massive, reinforced tunnel bridged the two fiercely divided territories, acting as a grim corridor between worlds.

Before he could approach the entrance, a disheveled woman stumbled out from the shadows of the barricades. Her clothes were torn, and she looked as though she had just narrowly escaped the mechanized city. When her eyes locked onto Kenjiro, all the color drained from her face. She dropped to her knees, trembling violently.

"Young master? Please... please don't take me back to the Emperor! Please have mercy. Let me go!"

"Who are you talking to?" Kenjiro asked, raising an eyebrow. "I've never seen you before."

The woman blinked, her frantic gaze searching his face. Realizing he wasn't going to strike her, or perhaps too consumed by her own terror to care, she scrambled to her feet and fled past him into the mist, disappearing as quickly as she had arrived.

Kenjiro adjusted the straps of his pack, not particularly bothered by the lady's panicked worries. However, the incident left a strange seed of confusion in his mind. 'Young master?' 'The Emperor?' None of it made sense.

Pushing the encounter aside, he stepped into the dark, echoing tunnel connecting the Infinity Palace to the Robot Metropolis. The air grew colder and more metallic the deeper he walked. As Kenjiro approached the far end of the tunnel, attempting to cross the final threshold into the sprawling mechanical city, a sudden, blinding pain pierced his skull.

He gasped, gripping his head as a flood of vivid, disjointed memories crashed over him—echoes of a life he had never lived, faces he had never met, and towering geometric structures he had never seen. The sensory overload was agonizing.

Instinctively, Kenjiro staggered backward, retreating several paces away from the city's border. The moment he stepped back, the intense headache vanished instantly, leaving him panting in the damp shadows of the tunnel.

"Let's take a break from this," Kame-kun, the single, small AI turtle resting securely on his shoulder, advised gently. His blue optics scanned Kenjiro's elevated heart rate. "Your neural pathways are experiencing acute rejection. We'll come back later."
