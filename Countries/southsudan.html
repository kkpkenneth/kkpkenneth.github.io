The Urban Landscape of Juba

Juba is a city that defies simple categorization, defined by profound contradictions. As you navigate the capital, the visual disparity is jarring: an imposing, pyramid-shaped 5-star hotel stands in immediate, suffocating proximity to an expansive, impoverished slum. This layout is a physical manifestation of the severe socio-economic volatility that grips the nation's center.

Cultural Documentation and Administrative Risks

The journey was motivated by a desire to document the vibrant cultural heritage of the Dinka tribe. However, the attempt to capture these images led to a harrowing experience. While conducting a photoshoot, I was detained by officials identified as being among the most corrupt police and military forces in the world. This encounter served as a stark lesson in the extreme risks and the hostile, unpredictable administrative environment that travelers face when attempting to engage with local populations in this complex region.
