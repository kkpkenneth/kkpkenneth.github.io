Baltic Soul

A journey through the architectural grandeur of Riga, ascending the heights of the Academy of Sciences observatory, and confronting a somber piece of history within the walls of a former prison.

Advertisement

The Architectural Gem: Riga

The capital, Riga, is an absolute masterpiece of Northern European architecture. Wandering its Old Town feels like walking through a living history book, where medieval buildings sit beside world-renowned Art Nouveau facades. It is a city that balances its rich, artistic past with a vibrant, modern energy.

Views from Above: The Academy of Sciences Observatory

For the most commanding perspective of the city, head to the observation deck of the Latvian Academy of Sciences. This imposing Stalinist-era skyscraper offers a panoramic view that perfectly captures Riga’s unique urban layout—from the historic spires of the Old Town to the winding flow of the Daugava River. It is a stark, impressive vantage point that bridges the gap between the city’s complex Soviet past and its bright, modern present.

A Confronting History: The Former Prison Experience

Stepping into a former prison in Riga is a deeply somber and necessary experience. These walls hold the echoes of a turbulent century, and the tour offers an unfiltered, confronting look at the realities of life for those held captive under past regimes. It is an unsettling, powerful encounter that provides a essential perspective on the resilience of the Latvian people and the importance of remembering these dark chapters of history.

A Taste of Tradition: Rye Bread and Grey Peas

No trip to Latvia is complete without sampling the foundational flavors of the Baltic. Latvian Rye Bread (rupjmaize) is dark, dense, and deeply aromatic, often served as a staple with every meal. Pair it with Grey Peas with Speck—a traditional, hearty dish of boiled local peas sautéed with crispy, smoked bacon and onions—for a culinary experience that is as grounded, rustic, and comforting as the Latvian countryside itself.
