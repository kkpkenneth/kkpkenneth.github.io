Countable & Uncountable Nouns

Countable Nouns: Can be singular or plural (car, cars). Use many, a few, a/an.
Uncountable Nouns: Cannot be counted directly and are usually singular (water, advice). Use much, a little, some. Never use a/an directly.
Common Uncountables: Information, luggage, furniture, homework, research, bread, news.
