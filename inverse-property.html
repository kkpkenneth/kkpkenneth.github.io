Inverse Property Rule Unlocked

Because exponents and logarithms are inverse functions, composing them cancels each other out when bases match!

1. blogb(x) = x (Base raised to a log with the same base leaves just the argument x)

2. logb(bx) = x (Logarithm of a base raised to an exponent leaves just the exponent x)
