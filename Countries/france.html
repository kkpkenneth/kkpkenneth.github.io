From the Pink Carpet to the Ends of the Earth

A grand tour sweeping from the iconic streets of Paris down to the glamour of the Côte d'Azur, and spanning across the globe to the incredible beauty of the French Overseas Territories.

Advertisement

The Capital & The Brittany Coast: Paris to St. Malo

The journey through metropolitan France began in the undeniable heart of it all—Paris. After taking in the romance and chaotic elegance of the capital, the route pushed northwest into the historic depths of Brittany. Wandering the medieval, timber-framed streets of Rennes set the tone before hitting the coast at St. Malo, a walled corsair city built from solid granite. Just across the sweeping tidal flats, witnessing the towering, gravity-defying abbey of Mont Saint Michel rising from the mist felt like stepping directly into a fantasy novel.

Gastronomy & Glamour: Lyon to Cannes

Heading south, the atmosphere shifts completely. Passing through Lyon, the undisputed culinary capital of France, the journey continued down to the Mediterranean coast to the raw, sun-baked port city of Marseille. The French Riviera (Côte d'Azur) offered pure escapism. Walking the vibrant promenades of Nice was incredible, but the absolute highlight was arriving in Cannes. In a truly surreal and unforgettable moment, I had the privilege of joining the Canneseries pink carpet event, soaking in the flashbulbs, the fashion, and the undeniable glamour of the international television festival.

The Eastern Crossroads: Strasbourg

Later in the journey, the path swung east to the Alsace region to visit Strasbourg. Sitting right on the border, this city is a masterpiece of cultural blending, where French elegance and German architectural charm seamlessly intertwine along the canals of the Grand Île and beneath the shadow of its magnificent gothic cathedral.

Beyond Europe: The Overseas Territories

To truly understand the scale of France, you have to leave Europe behind. My expeditions have taken me to the breathtaking French Overseas Territories (DOM-TOMs), each offering a wildly different ecosystem. I explored the dense, untamed Amazonian jungles of French Guiana in South America, soaked in the vibrant Creole culture and pristine beaches of the Caribbean in both Martinique and Guadeloupe, and hiked the dramatic, otherworldly volcanic landscapes of Reunion Island deep in the Indian Ocean. These territories showcase the vast, global diversity of the French Republic.

The Perfect Comfort: Ratatouille

France is world-renowned for its Michelin-starred restaurants and intricate, butter-heavy pastries, but amidst all the complex gastronomy, my absolute favorite dish remains a beautifully executed Ratatouille. There is something profoundly comforting about this classic Provençal stew. The rich, slow-simmered medley of tomatoes, zucchini, eggplant, and bell peppers, heavily fragrant with herbs and olive oil, perfectly captures the rustic, sun-drenched soul of southern France.
