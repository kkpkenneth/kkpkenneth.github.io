Plural Nouns Master Quiz

-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z: Add -es (bus → buses)
Consonant + Y: Change Y to I and add -es (city → cities)
Vowel + Y: Just add -s (boy → boys)
-f or -fe: Often change to -ves (wolf → wolves)
No Change: sheep, deer, species, series, fish
Irregulars: man → men, child → children, tooth → teeth
