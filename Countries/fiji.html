Fiji

Travel Guide & Archive

Bula: Exploring the Heart of the Pacific

A photographic journey through Fiji's urban hubs, cultural landmarks, and remote island reefs.

Advertisement

Fiji is a vibrant archipelago where deep-rooted Melanesian traditions meet a bustling modern Pacific lifestyle. Across multiple expeditions spanning 2019, 2023, and 2025, I explored the diverse landscapes of these islands. From the commercial pulse of Viti Levu to the pristine, coral-fringed shores of the Yasawa Islands, this archive captures the contrasting faces of a nation celebrated for its warmth and ecological beauty.

Nadi & Denarau: The Western Gateway

The journey into Fiji often begins in Nadi, the bustling heart of Viti Levu's western coast. The vibrant, multi-tiered architecture of the Sri Siva Subramaniya Swami Temple provides a colorful cultural anchor to the city. From the busy Nadi Bus Station to dining on local root vegetables at the Jetpoint Super Centre, everyday life is always in motion. Nearby, the Tifajek Mud Pool & Hotspring offers natural relaxation, while the Garden of the Sleeping Giant showcases lush tropical flora. Just a short trip away lies Denarau Island, where the Port Denarau Marina serves as the launching point for maritime adventures, bordered by luxury stays and beautiful beach swings.

Suva: Capital Culture and Heritage

Moving to the eastern side of Viti Levu, the capital city of Suva presents a striking contrast with its colonial architecture and lively streets. A visit to the Fiji Museum, set within the historic Thurston Gardens, offers a deep dive into the nation's past. The elegance of the Grand Pacific Hotel provides a serene retreat, while the energetic atmosphere of TappooCity and its food courts reveals the city's modern pulse. Dinners at Tikos Seafood Restaurant in Suva Harbour and watching the boats navigate Nabukalou Creek round out the urban experience. Unique wildlife encounters, such as spotting fruit bats hanging from trees, remind visitors that nature thrives alongside the city.

Pacific Harbour & The Coral Coast

Traveling along the southern coast brings you to Pacific Harbour and the district of Navua. The entrance to the Pacific Harbour Arts Village serves as a gateway to local crafts and culture. The coastal relaxation here is unparalleled, whether it's enjoying a milkshake at The Pearl Resort & Spa or resting in a beachfront room at the Uprising Beach Resort. Further along the coastline at places like Likuri Harbour and the peaceful shores of Rovodrau Bay, the rhythm of island life slows down entirely.

The Yasawa Islands: Drawaqa & Kuata

Leaving the mainland, the Yasawa Islands offer pristine remote beauty. Drawaqa Island welcomed me with the traditional 'Bula' spelled out on the bed, setting the tone for days of exploring vibrant underwater coral reefs. Nearby, Kuata provided stunning ocean views framed by lush greenery and traditional bungalows. It is here, among the clear waters and vibrant marine life, that the postcard-perfect image of the South Pacific truly comes to life.

This archive represents an ongoing exploration of Fiji's diverse islands, cultures, and marine ecosystems.
