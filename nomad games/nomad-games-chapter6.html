Chapter 6

The Growling Grasslands

The vast, endless expanse of the Growling Grasslands stretched out beneath a canopy of brilliant stars, completely empty save for the howling winds and a single, roaring bonfire.

"Ghen-ghen-ghenkiskhan!" Papa Bataar bellowed joyfully into the night sky, stomping his boots in time with the crackling flames.

Around the fire, the rest of the friendly Bataar family—Mama Bataar, Sister Bataar, and Brother Bataar—danced and sang along, their laughter carrying over the plains. Puppy Bataar, who was in fact a giant, thick-coated, black hairy dog the size of a small bear, barked happily, tackling Kenjiro with cuddly enthusiasm.

"I have to get stronger, Kame-kun," Kenjiro said, affectionately pushing the giant dog's heavy paws off his chest. He looked at the single, small AI turtle resting securely on his shoulder. "That psychic rejection at the border of the Robot Metropolis... it was because of my lack of strength. I need to awaken an Extraordinary Trait. An ET."

"This is the optimal location for such training," Kame-kun replied, his optical sensors scanning the dancing family. "The nomads of the Growling Grasslands specialize in wind-based telekinesis. Their empire was once one of the largest in the world. Though their global power has weakened over the centuries, they have traded conquest for a much more lively, free, and relaxed culture."

Determined, Kenjiro threw himself into their way of life. For weeks, he trained with the Bataars. He learned to build heavy fabric tents to withstand the biting gales. He wrestled bare-chested against Brother Bataar in the tall grass. He replenished his energy by eating towering stacks of crispy, hot, meat-filled khuushuur prepared by Mama Bataar.

"I could really do without the horseback riding, though," Kenjiro groaned one evening, rubbing his sore thighs as he limped back to the camp. "I really, really don't like horses."

Seeking spiritual guidance, the Bataars took him to pray at their ancient, open-air temples. Kneeling before the stone altars, Kenjiro closed his eyes, searching for the wind. But instead of feeling the breeze bending to his will, he felt a strange, echoing resonance in his mind—a whisper of thoughts that weren't his own. It was a subtle hint that the Extraordinary Trait dormant within him was something entirely different from wind telekinesis.

Yet, despite all the intense training, the wrestling, and the deep meditation, Kenjiro stood before the bonfire one evening, staring at his hands in defeat. He tried to push a small pebble with his mind. Nothing happened. He couldn't squeeze out even a single drop of magic, superpower, or extraordinary energy.

"It's useless," Kenjiro sighed, dropping his arms. "I have no ET at all."

Papa Bataar walked over, his warm smile crinkling the corners of his eyes. He reached out and clamped a heavy, comforting hand onto Kenjiro's bare shoulder.

"Do not force the wind, young brother," Papa Bataar rumbled. "It blows only when it knows it is welcome."

The exact second Papa Bataar's skin touched his, a jolt of pure, blinding clarity shocked Kenjiro's brain. He wasn't just hearing Papa Bataar's voice; he could hear the man's inner thoughts, feel his joyful memories, and, most importantly, sense the well of wind-based telekinesis humming in his blood.

"My ET... it isn't wind," Kenjiro gasped, his eyes flying open as he looked at Kame-kun. "It's Telepathy! I can link with his mind!"

Maintaining physical contact with Papa Bataar, Kenjiro focused his newly awakened telepathic link, temporarily "borrowing" the nomad's abilities. He thrust his free hand forward. A massive, telekinetic gale erupted from his palm, lifting the bonfire into a swirling, spectacular vortex of fire and wind that illuminated the entire grassland.

The Bataar family cheered in awe as Kenjiro held the flaming tornado aloft. For five glorious minutes, he commanded the sky, realizing that his true power wasn't just physical strength—it was the profound ability to connect with the strength of others.
