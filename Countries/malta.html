Mediterranean Jewel

An exploration of the historic capital of Valletta, the rugged landscapes of Gozo, the turquoise paradise of Comino, and the traditional luzzu boats—a territory defined by its deep maritime heritage, ancient stone architecture, and a highly distinctive, sun-drenched Mediterranean identity.

Advertisement

Historic Capital: Valletta

Valletta is a living museum, a UNESCO World Heritage site built by the Knights of St. John. The capital is defined by its massive fortified walls, narrow grid-patterned streets, and ornate baroque churches. Walking through the city, one feels the weight of history in the limestone facades, grand squares, and harbors that have served as the strategic gateway to the Mediterranean for centuries.

Island Life: Gozo & Comino

Gozo offers a more rural, pastoral experience, featuring ancient megalithic temples and dramatic coastline cliffs. Just a short hop away lies Comino, home to the iconic, intensely vibrant turquoise waters of the Blue Lagoon. Whether navigating the rugged trails of Gozo or swimming in the crystalline waters surrounding Comino, these islands provide a serene escape from the bustle of the main island.

Maritime Tradition: Luzzu Boats

No journey to Malta is complete without noting the traditional luzzu boats. These colorful, wooden vessels—often painted in bright shades of yellow, red, blue, and green—are a symbol of the islands’ deep maritime roots. Adorned with the "Eye of Osiris" on their bows, they remain an essential part of the Maltese landscape, ferrying travelers and fishermen across the sparkling Mediterranean waters with timeless charm.

Maltese Flavors

Maltese cuisine is a hearty reflection of its diverse history, blending Italian, North African, and British influences. From the savory pastizzi—flaky pastries filled with ricotta or peas—to fresh, Mediterranean-style rabbit stew and daily catches of lampuki, the food here is a simple, delicious celebration of local ingredients and the enduring traditions of an island nation set in the heart of the sea.
