The Western Edge of Europe

An extensive journey across Portugal's historic districts: the vibrant capital of Lisbon (Lisbon District), the fairy-tale landscapes of Sintra, the golden heritage of Évora (Évora District), and the coastal charm of Faro (Faro District).

Advertisement

Capital and Culture: Lisbon & Sintra

The journey begins in Lisbon, a city of seven hills defined by its iconic yellow trams, historic Fado music, and stunning views over the Tagus River. Just a short trip away, Sintra transports visitors to a world of fairy-tale palaces, mist-covered forests, and the colorful, hilltop Pena Palace, representing the romantic soul of the region.

History and Coast: Évora & Faro

Heading inland to the Alentejo region, Évora stands as a living museum, featuring a well-preserved Roman Temple and medieval streets that reflect centuries of Portuguese history. Finally, in the far south, Faro serves as the gateway to the Algarve. With its charming old town, historic marina, and beautiful coastal lagoons, it provides the perfect finish to a journey through the heart of Portugal.
