Digestive System

The sequential path food travels through the gastrointestinal (GI) tract.

  1. Mouth (Oral Cavity)

    Where digestion begins with chewing and salivary enzymes.

  2. Esophagus

    The muscular tube transporting food to the stomach via peristalsis.

  3. Stomach

    A J-shaped muscular sac that mixes food with acids to break it down into chyme.

  4. Small Intestine

    A long, winding tube where the majority of nutrient absorption takes place.

  5. Large Intestine

    Absorbs water and electrolytes. Includes the Ascending, Transverse, and Descending Colon.

  6. Rectum & Anus

    Stores and expels solid waste from the body.

Accessory Organs

  • Liver

    Produces bile to help digest fats.

  • Gallbladder

    Stores and concentrates bile from the liver.

  • Pancreas

    Produces digestive enzymes and neutralizes stomach acid.
