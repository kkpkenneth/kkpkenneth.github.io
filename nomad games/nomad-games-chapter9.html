Chapter 9

The Clock Tower

A steady, light shower drummed against the cobblestone paths as Kenjiro navigated the rain-slicked streets of the capital. Towering above the city's historic skyline stood the magnificent Grand Chronometer, holding the legendary bell known simply as the Great Chime. Its golden clock face glowed brightly through the gentle morning drizzle, casting a warm reflection over the puddles on the central iron bridge.

"Now this is a change of pace from the crystal chandeliers of Luckland," Kenjiro remarked, pulling his coat tighter against the brisk, wet wind. He glanced sideways at the plush size AI turtle perching on his shoulder.

"Indeed," Kame-kun whirred quietly, a tiny, built-in energy shield repelling the raindrops from his chassis. "My atmospheric sensors detect continuous light precipitation, and historical data indicates we are standing near the political and temporal center of the realm. It is highly probable that powerful ET users congregate in areas with such dense historical resonance."

Before hunting for any potential threats or allies, Kenjiro's stomach let out a loud, undeniable growl. Taking Kame-kun's advice to get out of the rain and blend in, they stopped at a bustling corner pub near the edge of the river.

Kenjiro eagerly devoured a steaming, newspaper-wrapped portion of traditional fish and chips. The battered cod was perfectly crisp, heavily dashed with sharp malt vinegar, and served alongside thick-cut chips that radiated warmth into his chilled hands.

"Incredible. The vinegar cuts right through the richness," Kenjiro mumbled happily with a full mouth, wiping grease from his chin.

"A robust choice for a damp morning, young sir, though it is usually preferred with a side of mushy peas," a crisp, exceedingly polite voice echoed from a few paces away.

Kenjiro turned to see a tall, immaculate gentleman leaning lightly on a silver-tipped walking umbrella. He wore a perfectly tailored tweed three-piece suit, a crisp pocket square, and a silver pocket watch chain draped across his waistcoat. In his gloved hand, he effortlessly balanced a bone-china teacup filled with steaming Earl Grey. Resting right beside his teacup on the wooden table was a plush-sized navigation AI—an adorable, fluffy plush bear pilot with pristine digital optics.

"Barometric pressure and humidity parameters indicate optimal tea-steeping conditions," the plush bear pilot chirped, its soft voice carrying a precise digital cadence. "Though your posture deviation suggests minor fatigue, sir."

"Uh, thanks. I'm Kenjiro," he said, swallowing quickly and standing up.

"Arthur Pendelton, at your service, and this is Pip," the gentleman said, giving a slight, elegant bow. His sharp blue eyes flicked immediately to the plush size AI turtle perching on Kenjiro's shoulder. "I see you travel with a rather unique companion. And a heavy aura. You are a Telepath, are you not?"

Kenjiro tensed, immediately dropping his half-eaten chip. If this man could sense his Extraordinary Trait without even making physical contact, he was incredibly dangerous.

"Peace, my boy," Arthur chuckled softly, taking a refined sip of his tea. "I have no quarrel with you. I am simply a man who watches the clock. You see, the flow of time in this city is my domain. My ET allows me to perceive the temporal ripples of those destined to alter the future."

"Temporal perception is theoretically impossible without violating the laws of thermodynamics," Kame-kun interjected skeptically.

"Logical assessment," Pip the bear pilot agreed, its plush ears twitching slightly. "Yet temporal anomaly integration remains a fascinating variable."

Arthur smiled, reaching into his pocket and pulling out his silver watch. He didn't press a button, but merely ran his thumb over the glass. In the blink of an eye, the steady shower of rain outside froze completely in mid-air. The bustling crowds on the iron bridge paused like statues. The only things moving were Arthur, Pip, Kenjiro, and Kame-kun.

"You stopped time..." Kenjiro whispered in awe, poking a suspended raindrop that hovered just outside the pub's awning.

"Only for three seconds. A gentleman never overstays his welcome in the fabric of the universe," Arthur noted lightly as the world suddenly slammed back into motion, the rain splashing down onto the cobblestones in a heavy wave. "I approached you, Kenjiro, because your path is about to intersect with deeply dangerous individuals. There are others seeking the secrets you hold. Seek the vaults beneath the Grand Archives. You will find the answers you need there, before the hour strikes."

Before Kenjiro could ask for clarification, the Great Chime began to toll, its massive, booming sound echoing across the river through the rain. Dong. Dong. Kenjiro instinctively looked up at the great clock face. When he looked back down, Arthur Pendelton and Pip were entirely gone, leaving behind only the faint, lingering scent of bergamot tea in the damp city air.
