Azerbaijan

Fire and Silk

Situated at the vital crossroads of Eastern Europe and Western Asia, Azerbaijan serves as a living museum of the ancient Silk Road. From the modern, sweeping curves of Baku's architecture to the timeless practices preserved in its alleyways, the country demands to be decoded layer by layer.

Heritage & Architecture

The juxtaposition of eras here is striking. Wandering through the urban landscape reveals a rapid evolution of engineering and design, yet the foundational roots of these settlements—often tied to the region's unique geological fire phenomena—remain deeply embedded in the cultural identity.

Culinary Anthropology

To truly understand a region along the ancient trade routes, one must look at how they break bread. The traditional tandoor baking methods observed here are not merely food preparation; they are a centuries-old social anchor. The rhythmic slapping of dough against the hot clay walls of a local bakery is a practice practically unchanged since the days of the early caravans.

Observing traditional bread preparation in a local bakery.

Of course, no exploration is complete without examining the modern intersections of culture. Stepping into establishments like the Beer Station Pub provides a contrasting, contemporary perspective on social gathering in the city today.
