The Capital Pulse: Moscow

Moscow is the beating heart of Russia, a city where centuries of history meet rapid modern development. From the iconic red walls and golden domes of the Kremlin and Red Square to the grand Stalinist skyscrapers that loom over the city, Moscow serves as the political, economic, and cultural center of the nation. It is a sprawling metropolis of immense scale, famous for its world-class art, theater, and the intricate, palatial design of its historic metro system.

Imperial Elegance: St. Petersburg

Known as the "Venice of the North," St. Petersburg is the cultural and imperial jewel of Russia. Built by Peter the Great on a network of canals and islands, the city showcases stunning Baroque and Neoclassical architecture. It is home to the world-renowned Hermitage Museum, the historic Winter Palace, and a lifestyle defined by literature, ballet, and the ethereal light of the White Nights. It stands in stark, elegant contrast to the raw power and intensity of Moscow.
